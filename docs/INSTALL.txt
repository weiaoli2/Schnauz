Schnauz 0.5.5 — early preview

Drag Schnauz.app to Applications, then open it from there.
Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Download the correct Apple Silicon or Intel build.

This preview is signed with Oliver's Apple Development certificate, but is not
Apple-notarized. macOS may block the first launch. After trying to open the app,
use System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway if you trust the download.
Do not disable Gatekeeper. Apple Developer ID signing and notarization are planned.

The first-launch file-access guide explains optional Full Disk Access for protected
folders. macOS controls these permissions. If upgrading from an earlier ad-hoc
build, you may need to re-add the current app in Full Disk Access once.
